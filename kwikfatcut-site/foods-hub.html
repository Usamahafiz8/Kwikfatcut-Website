Pot Talk — the yard before the science.

Every food profile opens the way it would be spoken at home: the Yaad (Yard) Name, then the Aroma, then the Flavor — in warm, dignified Patwa (Patois) — before the plain-English teaching begins. The culture first, then the understanding. That is Pot Talk.
